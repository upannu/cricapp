CRIC HQ — Database ER Diagram

All 24 Postgres tables (via Supabase) backing the platform, grouped by domain: roster & org, payments, coaching sessions, group sessions & attendance, coach workflow, and academy learning content. Generated directly from web/lib/db.ts's Db* interfaces and .from("...") calls — reflects the real, current schema.

Note on players ↔ academies: there is no direct academy_id column on players. An academy's roster instead lives as a player_ids array on the academies row itself, and a player's academy is otherwise inferred via their assigned coach's academy_id. This diagram shows the real foreign-key path (academies → coaches → players) rather than a FK that doesn't actually exist in the schema.
